About the Ugandan BTVET System (UVTAB)

The Business, Technical, and Vocational Education and Training (BTVET) system in Uganda is designed to equip learners with practical skills for the labor market. As a UVTAB-registered institute, Avitech adheres to the national curriculum standards.

Upon completion of your course, you will be awarded a certificate that is recognized by employers across Uganda and beyond.
