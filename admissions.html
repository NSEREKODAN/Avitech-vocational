How to Apply

Step 1: Choose a Course

Select from our 6 practical vocational courses. Review the fees structure.

Step 2: Visit or Call

Visit our campus at Mawale along Semuto Kampala Road, or call/WhatsApp us at 0708796916.

Step 3: Submit Documents

Bring a copy of your ID, passport photos, and previous academic documents (if any).

Step 4: Pay & Enroll

Pay your admission fee (installments allowed) and officially begin your hands-on training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are installments allowed for tuition?
Yes! We understand that education is an investment. Avitech allows students to pay their tuition in flexible installments. Please discuss a payment plan with our admissions office upon registration.
Do I need prior experience to join?
No prior experience is required. Our courses are designed to take beginners from the ground up, focusing heavily on practical, hands-on training.
Is the certificate recognized?
Absolutely. Avitech is UVTAB registered, meaning our certificates meet the national standards for vocational training in Uganda. They are recognized by employers across the country.
What is included in the total fees?
The total fees include admission, examination, student ID, development, medical checkup, medical card, training materials, and overall administration costs. (Note: Motorvehicle mechanics has an extra 100,000 UGX for fuel).
