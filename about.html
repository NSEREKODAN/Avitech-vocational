About Avitech

Our Story

Avitech Vocational Training Institute is a UVTAB-registered institution located in Mawale along the Semuto Kampala Road. We were founded with the vision to bridge the skills gap in Uganda by providing high-quality, practical vocational training to the youth.

We believe in "Success Beyond Hardwork." Our curriculum is designed to not only teach technical skills but also to instill discipline, entrepreneurship, and problem-solving abilities in our students.

Our Vision

To be the leading center of vocational excellence in Uganda, producing skilled professionals who drive economic development.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and industry-relevant training to empower individuals with the skills needed for self-reliance and employment.
